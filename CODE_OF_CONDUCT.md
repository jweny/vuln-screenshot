# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of technical expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing the technical issue directly.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's credentials, captured traffic, or personal information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or project infrastructure.
- Using the project community to coordinate unauthorized security testing.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through a contact method listed on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/jweny). Reports will be reviewed confidentially and handled in proportion to their impact.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when this code is violated.
